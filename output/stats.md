# Statistical notes

## Market size
- Last 12 months to 2026-03: UK online whole market estimate CAD 15,759M, Ontario CAD 4,266M (27% of the UK figure).
- Per adult in the last 12 months: UK CAD 24.36 per month, Ontario CAD 28.21. Ontario has been above the UK per adult every month since 2025-09.
- A year earlier (Apr 2023 to Mar 2024) Ontario was at CAD 16.13 per adult against UK CAD 19.14.

## Product mix
- UK: slots went from 40% of online GGY in 2021 to 51% in 2025; betting fell from 45% to 38%.
- Ontario: casino went from 65% of NAGGR in 2022 to 80% in 2026; betting hold rose from 6.3% to 7.2% of wagers.

## Monetization
- Last 12 months: Ontario ARPPA CAD 304 per account per month; UK slots and casino GGY per active CAD 80; ratio 3.8x. UK actives are counted per vertical and one person can appear in several, so the UK figure is a lower bound per person and the gap is partly definitional.

## UK slots and the 2025 stake limits (10 months before, Jun 2024 to Mar 2025, against 10 months after, Jun 2025 to Mar 2026)
- Sessions over one hour: 6.00% to 4.50% of sessions (-1.50 pp, p < 0.001). At the after period's volume that is about 9.9 million fewer long sessions over ten months.
- GGY per 100 spins: GBP 2.935 to 3.064 (+4.4%, p < 0.001).
- Year on year growth of slots GGY: +13.7% in the ten months before the limits, +10.7% in the ten months after (p = 0.092). Slots GGY kept growing; the limits changed session length, not revenue.

## Seasonality (index against the centred 12-month average, 100 = average)
- UK betting: peak in March (126), trough in September (77); slots stay within 92 to 108.
- Ontario betting: peak 133 in month 1, trough 80 in month 8; casino 95 to 107.

## Ontario forecast (fit on Apr 2024 to Sep 2026, log-linear trend plus month effects)
- Underlying growth 2.18% per month, about 30% a year; residual standard deviation 5.0% of the level.
- Next 12 months (Oct 2026 to Sep 2027): CAD 6,178M, 80% interval CAD 5,794M to 6,587M, against CAD 4,777M in the last 12 actual months.
- At Ontario's 20% tax on iGaming revenue that is roughly CAD 1,236M of provincial revenue over the year, if the trend holds.

## UK lockdown, spring 2020 (GGY by product, March 2020 as the base)
- April 2020 against March: real-event betting -62% (GBP 160M to 62M), betting actives 4.63M to 2.07M; whole online market -20%.
- Other products gained GBP 16M in April against a betting shortfall of GBP 98M: casino +23%, poker +67%, slots -7%. Substitution covered 16% of the loss.
- June 2020, first full month with football back: real-event betting +36% against March, actives 4.04M, whole market +11%. Poker and casino went back to where they were within two months.

## Ontario betting hold volatility (hold = NAGGR / cash wagers)
- Betting hold averages 6.55% with a standard deviation of 1.30 points, a coefficient of variation of 20%; range 3.5% to 9.1%. Casino hold averages 3.56% with a standard deviation of 0.20 points.
- Against the trailing 12-month hold, the monthly result effect averages CAD 11M in either direction, from CAD -36M (2024-12) to +CAD 30M (2025-12). On the last twelve months of wagers (CAD 12,742M) one point of hold is worth CAD 127M a year.
- Over any twelve-month window the result effect nets to between CAD -61M and +CAD 63M, so an annual betting revenue plan built on the trailing hold carries roughly ±5% of results noise at 80% confidence.
- Annual hold has risen from 6.32% in 2022 to 7.17% in 2026, about 0.23 points a year, consistent with a growing share of parlays and same-game bets.
